01 / About

Building systems that do the busywork.

I'm Abrish Fatima, and I focus on AI automation and workflow building. My work sits where tools, data and AI models meet — figuring out how information should move between them, and building the workflows that make it happen automatically.

I work with platforms like n8n to orchestrate multi-step processes, with Claude to add reasoning and language understanding into those processes, and with APIs and FastAPI to connect services and build lightweight custom endpoints when an off-the-shelf integration isn't enough.

I care about workflows that are clear, maintainable and dependable — not just ones that work once in a demo.
